# Twelve sacred destinations

The former circular temple arrangement is replaced by twelve separate sites across a 500m terrain, with walkable bounds at ±235m. The Great Hall remains at (0,0), with four cardinal doorways. Wayfarer Hall is rebuilt with loggias, a ceremonial entrance, copper-coloured tiled roofs, a compass relief and a beacon tower.

Placement, region names, heights and trail waypoints share lib/game/sanctuary-layout.ts. Terrain blends into level temple terraces. Roads and vegetation reservations use the same sites. Detailed interiors remain separate pages; doorway triggers and return positions follow the relocated buildings.

Press M to open the new map. Select a temple marker to visit its exterior grounds. The temple guide continues to provide direct interior access. Visit Wayfarer Hall and Visit the Great Hall are available on the welcome screen.

Architecture is assembled once and batched by material. Full-detail temple buildings plus their grounds stay below 100,000 triangles per site. Water surfaces use a subtle animated shader. Existing distant vegetation optimizations remain, with High graphics throughout.

Checks cover all twelve ramps, portals and solid walls, four complete Great Hall crossings, Wayfarer's doorway and wall, and the Poseidon causeway in both directions. Blender renders inspect exported game geometry; they are not browser screenshots. Target-device GPU performance and browser visual acceptance remain pending.
