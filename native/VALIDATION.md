# Native camp checkpoint verification — 14 November 2026

Verified using Godot **4.7.2 stable**, Windows and the **OpenGL Compatibility** renderer on **Intel Iris Xe**.

- The original camp scenery, collision and shared-mesh GLBs import in the actual engine. The separate boar still exposes Idle, Walk and TuskAttack clips.
- **26 gameplay assertions passed**: camp/forest loading, physical grounding and eye height, walking, jumping and landing, pause, gate range, previous-region release, animation presence, disabled forest combat and damage/rewards, a passive unharmed creature, saving and reloading, return to camp, one active world, healing and the 24/8-minute cycle boundaries.
- **57 camp assertions passed**: twelve temple sites, the full camp extent, original instanced planting, the native water material, safe arrivals at eighteen destinations, twelve inscriptions and correct camera facing, Poseidon's causeway height, four open Great Hall doorways, map pause/travel/close behavior, Android Back handling and night/day lamp switching.
- **26 display-driven touch assertions passed**: five controls fit the viewport; joystick movement and simultaneous camera look; camera release over a button; jumping, striking and running while movement stays held; clean release; action taps; touch map opening and travel to Poseidon; no weapon/attack control outside practice; equipping on entry; immediate sheathing after travel; touch Bow selection, drawing while moving, release cleanup, visible charging and exactly one physical arrow on release.
- **27 polish assertions passed**: weapon state at every destination; disabled attacks outside practice; restored imported vertex colors; fitted, depth-tested architectural inscriptions; the bundled fonts; an actual sword hit on the existing practice dummy; cancelled swings on exit; and no economy rewards from practice.
- **44 training assertions passed**: real arrow flight into the existing middle bullseye and an outer ring; draw/string movement; lodged arrows; duplicate-hit prevention; flight pause/resume; a physical wall within the muzzle offset; five actual bullseyes completing a 50-point round; a blocked sixth shot; best-score save/reload; expired shots resolving as misses; weapon-switch cancellation; three actual sword hits and their combo bonus; a missed swing; timer pause and completion; map draw cancellation; immediate exit cleanup; no outside-yard attacks or economy rewards; damaged-save handling and practice-record validation.
- **180 assertions passed in total**, with no script errors or exit leak warnings in the final gameplay checks. Some managed Windows headless launches report certificate-store or log-directory access diagnostics before gameplay; the game makes no network requests. Region scripts are compiled on the main thread before heavy resources load in the background; this removed a reproducible six-instance warning in Godot 4.7.2's threaded script-loading path.
- Eight camp views were rendered and inspected at **1280 × 720** with phone controls: Arrival Ridge, Great Hall, Wayfarer Hall, training yard, lakeside, Poseidon, Ares and Hephaestus. Each reached the configured **30 FPS cap** after warm-up in the final run. The interactive map and night lighting were also captured. These short samples are not a sustained benchmark or Android result.
- Shared plant/detail prototypes were reduced from **91 to 48** by comparing geometry and material content. All **77,602** source instance placements remain, grouped into **4,411** spatial batches. Nearby undergrowth remains detailed; distance culling and automatic mesh LOD control rendering cost. Zero-energy night lights are hidden during daytime.
- Asset byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `assets-manifest.json`. These are source payload sizes, not runtime RAM usage or APK size.
- The Android preset includes the layout JSON explicitly. Android export was previously blocked by missing matching export templates and Java/Android SDK configuration; the user is setting these up. No installable APK is included in this checkpoint.
- Save serialization and file replacement were tested using isolated project files. Android device storage and actual pause/resume behavior still require a physical-device run.

The white training-yard wash was reproduced at ground level and traced to imported meshes with vertex-color data but `vertex_color_use_as_albedo` disabled. The original sand/plant colors are now applied to static and instanced meshes, with softer nonmetallic highlights. Ground-level training, Great Hall, Poseidon and Ares captures verify the change and reached the 30 FPS cap in the updated desktop preview. Cinzel inscriptions use measured glyph bounds and respect wall depth. All twelve temple names have mounted plaques ahead of the decorative frieze ribs so those ribs cannot occlude the text. The HUD uses a bundled condensed face, readable backplates and compact timed notices.

Three new training views were rendered and inspected at 1280 × 720 with touch controls: the sword-combo lane, the fully drawn ash bow and a completed 50-point archery drill. The sampled frame rates were 25–26 FPS on Iris Xe while other checks were running; this is not an Android benchmark. The imported sword, bow and arrow add about 66 KiB of GLB payload. Physical target shapes align with the existing straw faces. Arrow collision uses swept ray segments, gravity and a sixteen-arrow limit; scoring requires a front-facing target impact. Drill records persist without changing gold or XP.

## Current limits

The camp exterior layout is transferred, including the original hills, paved paths, twelve decorated temples, Great Hall, Wayfarer Hall, dining pavilion, gardens, training yard, lake, dock, pavilion, causeway and planting. Sword/bow practice and drills are functional. Temple interiors, quests and the large deep-forest landscape remain to be migrated. The separate 96-metre forest remains an encounter test region. Browser-specific volumetric clouds, localized mist shaders, plant wind and advanced water reflections are not yet fully reproduced in Godot.

The weapons use the existing simple practice designs; animated hands and combat audio are not included. The forest boar is currently passive because combat is restricted to the training ground. Navigation, future forest encounters and death/charge animations remain future work.

No physical Samsung, Android lifecycle or APK test has been performed. Release signing, store publishing and production performance acceptance remain outstanding.
