## Scenery and loading optimization checkpoint

The supplied build preserves the original models, textures, white limestone roads, 24-minute day, 8-minute night, sun, moon and night lamps. Opaque scenery now uses 88 spatial palette groups plus 58 retained special meshes: 146 meshes instead of 290. Four shared palette materials retain each surface color, roughness and metallic finish; identical architectural texture payloads share their material. All 985,778 source scenery triangles remain. The source GLB decreases from 103,671,552 to 77,606,036 bytes through indexing and rebatching. Ground, roads, water, transparent effects and emissive decoration keep their own materials. Architecture LOD bias is 0.65 Balanced / 0.85 High; nearby source detail remains available.

Collision baking produces a 4,135,917-byte compressed physics scene containing all 61 original chunks and exactly 475,218 original collision triangles. The bake verifies every restored face and transform against the imported collision source. Android loads the physics scene directly; the original collision GLB is excluded from the APK and retained in the editable archive. Loading references are released before preparation, and covered scenery is hidden during loading. Light and sky updates occur every 0.25 seconds while the clock and movement continue each frame.

More aggressive forest submissions, camera filtering and bulk-buffer experiments were rejected after foreground low-memory terminations on the SM-T515, despite working in the laptop preview. The delivered tree streamer retains the previously working 96-metre coarse cells and original per-instance upload method. Closing other tablet apps allowed the conservative build to complete loading. This does not establish stability under background memory pressure.

The conservative optimized diagnostic build at Arrival Ridge reports about 555 draw calls, 757,200 primitives and 135.44 MiB tracked graphics allocations on Balanced, compared with approximately 670 calls, 743,018 primitives and 154.56 MiB in the preceding diagnostic checkpoint. Reduced draw calls and allocation size did not produce a measurable frame-rate improvement on this tablet. A fresh SurfaceFlinger buffer measured 24 intervals in 7.806 seconds: **3.075 FPS**. A memory snapshot reported total PSS 274,975 kB and swap PSS 197,492 kB. These short samples are not a sustained benchmark. The current build still fails the comfortable-play target on this 2 GB tablet; no performance claim is made for untested 4/8 GB phones.
