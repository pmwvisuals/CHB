Captures verify the training ground, safe-zone weapon state, readable fonts, map opening, map travel to training, joystick movement and bow selection. No fatal Godot or script error appeared in the process log during this check. Four hardware-format warnings report automatic RGBAFloat-to-RGBAHalf conversion. A full bow scoring drill, sword combo drill, jump-height measurement, device save/reload and sustained lifecycle tests are still outstanding. The longer sword-practice status line also reaches beyond its panel on this display and needs fitting.

Performance is **not acceptable for play yet**. A second short sample near training gave 2.557 FPS. These are view-specific samples from a debug build at the tablet's 1920×1200 display size, not a sustained benchmark or a result for all Android devices. A camp-load memory snapshot reported total PSS 460,919 kB, swap PSS 363,172 kB and graphics 54,024 kB. Optimization should first measure visible geometry, draw calls, foliage overdraw, shadow cost and scene preparation; current samples do not isolate which cost dominates.
