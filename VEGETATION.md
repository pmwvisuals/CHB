# Asterion vegetation family

Fifteen Blender assets are generated by `scripts/blender/build_vegetation.py` using Blender 5.2.3 LTS. Trees use connected tapered tubes, grounded root collars, folded geometry leaves and recessed crown clusters.

| Setting | Trees | Bushes | Ground cover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forest | Woodland oak (4.6 m), woodland fir (5 m), midnight hornbeam (5.4 m), shadow cedar (5.7 m), deepwood spruce (6 m) | Woodland fern, tangled bramble | Dense dark grasses, taller wisps, bracken, fallen leaves |
| Sanctuary | Silver courtyard olive (3.6 m), blush flowering tree (3.3 m) | Laurel, flowering myrtle | Soft grass, ivory meadow flowers |

Heights describe the source models. Placed models have additional scale variation. The contact sheet enlarges smaller assets for inspection and is not a scale comparison. These are procedural botanical interpretations, not scanned species replicas.

## Rebuild

Run from the project root, with Blender and Sapling installed:

```powershell
& 'C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe' --background --python scripts/blender/build_vegetation.py -- 'ABSOLUTE_PROJECT_DIRECTORY'
```

The generator writes editable Blender and GLB exports, a leaf atlas, catalog and rendered contact sheet under `assets-source/vegetation/`. Generated runtime geometry is tracked in `lib/game/generated/vegetation.ts`. The editable sources and standard model exports are supplied in `asterion-vegetation-assets.zip`; generated source artifacts are ignored by Git.

The Blender scene includes a contact-sheet layout with presentation transforms. The individual GLB exports and runtime geometry use their intended meter scale at the origin. Leaf image textures are packed in the Blender file and GLBs. Blender's procedural bark shader is retained in the Blender source; GLB consumers may need to recreate its bump detail. The web runtime has its own procedural bark texture and bump map.

## Runtime

Trees and bushes use shared geometries across spatial batches. Individual leaves have folded, pointed geometry silhouettes. The solid recessed crown stays present at distance, preventing texture mipmaps and alpha testing from stripping the canopy. Branch radii taper from connected joints rather than producing spikes. Root collars remain intact in both distance representations and are embedded slightly into the terrain. Small leaf motion provides wind detail.

Nearby plants have full geometry; distant plants retain a continuous crown with reduced small-leaf geometry; ground cover is culled sooner. The game always uses High graphics quality. There are no Low/Medium menu presets. Geometry is embedded, so portable HTML requires no separate model downloads. Each source tree remains below 35,000 triangles; instances share geometry.

The forest border uses 5,200 trees across five dark varieties. New varieties are slightly taller at 5.4–6 m. Continuous random sampling with variable minimum spacing replaces the old jittered grid; shared growth patches create groves and clearings. Temple groves also use random scatter rather than concentric planting rings. The seed is fixed so the layout stays stable between visits.

`forest-understory.ts` places 112,000 grass clumps and 18,000 bushes, ferns, bracken and litter plants. Density and plant heights follow the same patch field as the tree belt. Roads and temple grounds remain clear. Each blade clump follows the terrain normal. Grass is batched into spatial cells and drawn nearby, while shrubs use their normal automatic distance geometry.

`forest-mist.ts` integrates a drifting 3D density field between the camera and scene depth. Mist builds through the forest; foreground buildings and trunks occlude it. The existing mist is preserved.

## Landscape polish

The entire camp and expedition use shared rolling terrain with matching collision geometry. Gentle hills blend back to level temple foundations and service areas; small knolls break up the central green. The former solid courtyard colour discs are removed. Paths conform to the terrain and fade into rough edges.

A coherent habitat field controls soil colour, grass density and bush clusters, leaving genuine bare-earth clearings between planted areas. Soil has fine grain and bump detail. Rocks have chunky weathered forms, softened corners, colour variation and moss, with shared geometry and solid collision for larger boulders.

The forest sample and expedition forest also mix all five forest tree types. The sanctuary retains its two smaller ornamental trees. Choose **Visit the camp gardens** or **Visit the woodland sample** from the welcome screen to compare them.

Validation includes decoded geometry, indices, revised heights, retained roots and distant crowns, distance culling, existing walking routes, and walking/jumping over hills. Blender renders were inspected. Browser visuals and GPU performance remain unverified: the embedded preview timed out and native browser automation could not verify the local-file URL. Publication status is reported with each delivery.
