# Asterion vegetation family

Twelve Blender assets are generated by `scripts/blender/build_vegetation.py` using Blender 5.2.3 LTS and Sapling Tree Gen 1.0.0. The generator relies on the installed Sapling extension; the extension itself is not included in this repository.

| Setting | Trees | Bushes | Ground cover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forest | Ancient shadow oak (28.4 m), towering dusk fir (27 m) | Woodland fern, tangled bramble | Low bracken, fallen leaves |
| Sanctuary | Silver courtyard olive (8.6 m), blush flowering tree (6 m) | Laurel, flowering myrtle | Soft grass, ivory meadow flowers |

Heights describe the source models. Placed models have additional scale variation. The contact sheet enlarges smaller assets for inspection and is not a scale comparison. These are procedural botanical interpretations, not scanned species replicas.

## Rebuild

Run from the project root, with Blender and Sapling installed:

```powershell
& 'C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe' --background --python scripts/blender/build_vegetation.py -- 'ABSOLUTE_PROJECT_DIRECTORY'
```

The generator writes editable Blender and GLB exports, a leaf atlas, catalog and rendered contact sheet under `assets-source/vegetation/`. Generated runtime geometry is tracked in `lib/game/generated/vegetation.ts`. The editable sources and standard model exports are supplied in `asterion-vegetation-assets.zip`; generated source artifacts are ignored by Git.

The Blender scene includes a contact-sheet layout with presentation transforms. The individual GLB exports and runtime geometry use their intended meter scale at the origin. Leaf image textures are packed in the Blender file and GLBs. Blender's procedural bark shader is retained in the Blender source; GLB consumers may need to recreate its bump detail. The web runtime has its own procedural bark texture and bump map.

## Runtime

Trees and bushes use one geometry per material and detail level, reused across spatial batches. Leaf clusters use alpha testing and matching cutout shadow materials. Nearby plants have full geometry; distant plants have reduced geometry; ground cover is culled sooner. Low graphics uses the simpler plant meshes. The leaf atlas and geometry are embedded, so the portable HTML pages require no separate model downloads.

The forest sample and expedition forest use the two large tree types and forest undergrowth. The sanctuary has the two smaller trees and ornamental planting. Choose **Visit the camp gardens** or **Visit the woodland sample** from the welcome screen to compare them.

Validation includes decoded geometry, indices, heights, smaller distance meshes, quality switching, distance culling and the existing walking-route tests. Blender renders were inspected.
