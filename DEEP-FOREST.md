# The deep forest landscape

The supplied Magic Wolf now occupies the hollow clearing, the mutant mantis the ridge clearing, and the fantasy blue boar the western clearing. One of each accompanies the existing Ash-Tusk Boar. Wolf and blue boar alternate walking and idle pauses along short routes, while the mantis plays its single included loop at its habitat. They use the forest's current peaceful behaviour. Animation and rendering suspend at distance. The prepared files retain their supplied textures and animations; pose-aware scale correction resolves their different source units.

Prepared sources are retained under `assets-source/imported-creatures/`; rebuild with `scripts/blender/prepare_forest_creatures.py`, then import the native project in Godot.

The native Ash-Tusk Boar now roams the first clearing along the main trail, around 90 metres from the arrival point. Its six-point route alternates slow walking and idle pauses. It pauses near the player. The actor uses the existing rigged game model and distance levels, with physical grounding throughout the route. The current creature placement uses peaceful behaviour. A saved player position beside its normal spawn makes it appear on the other side of the clearing when re-entering.

Native forest trees now use a darker evergreen and bark palette. A repeatable scatter mixes irregular groves with looser background woodland and variable trunk separation. Tree count, species and sizes stay consistent with the first landscape pass. Nearby safe-position recovery handles a saved player position that overlaps a relocated trunk.

The native Godot forest now has a 550-metre playable diameter, with surrounding tree scenery out to 330 metres from its centre. The first landscape pass reuses the existing forest terrain and trail design, all five dark tree varieties, and woodland floor assets. It includes 5,200 tall trees, 20,000 litter/fern/bracken/bramble placements, four connected earth trails, six clearings, low hills and hollows. The broader native boundary includes the entire eastern trail endpoint.

From the native camp map, travel to **Deepwood Gate**, then use **E** or the interaction button to enter. The stone gate behind the forest arrival returns to camp. Walking, sprinting, jumping, touch controls and both existing graphics choices work through the normal game controls. This checkpoint develops landscape exploration. The existing boar is passive near the first clearing.

Tree meshes are shared across spatial instance groups. Near leaves have bounded detail distances, distant crowns reuse the existing foliage streamer, and trunk collisions are created around the player and released farther away. Terrain rendering and physics share the same triangle grid. The canopy uses real directional shadows and restrained cool fill so the ground stays readable. Twelve soft, depth-tested mist pockets occupy hollows, with denser haze near the outer boundary. The forest's large prototype resource loads after the previous region is released. Old test-forest saves relocate to the new entrance once, with progression retained.

Rebuild native forest assets with `node scripts/export-native-forest.mjs`, using the original vegetation source GLBs under `assets-source/vegetation/`, then import the native project in Godot. ## Browser landscape

Choose **Explore the deep forest** on the sanctuary welcome screen or pause menu. This opens `forest.html?view=landscape`: landscape-only exploration with no enemies, loot, weapon models or expedition-save changes. The earlier combat demo remains available through Quests & expeditions. The shared forest page and portable package keep their existing filenames.

The playable woodland spans roughly 520 metres, surrounded by further scenery out to a 330-metre radius. Rolling ground, a low ridge, depressions and several small clearings break up the landscape. Four connected winding earth trails cross the interior. There are no stone road borders, flower beds, ornamental trees, decorative structures or new creature assets.

5,200 instances reuse all five existing dark forest tree models. Trees are scaled to approximately 13–22 metres high, with slightly broader crowns so neighbouring canopies overlap. The lowest foliage stays above normal standing eye height. Trunks and rocks have collision; leaves do not act as movement barriers. Root collars are embedded using the local slope, and the ground mesh is shared by rendering and collision. Trees use deterministic irregular scatter, clear trails and reserved sky openings instead of planting rows.

The floor uses mottled soil and restrained moss detail, 20,000 reused leaf-litter/fern/bracken/bramble instances, and 170 existing weathered rocks. Shrubs remain low. There is no meadow-grass carpet or flower planting. Distance batching and the existing tree distance geometry keep the large field bounded without introducing graphics quality menus.

Actual tree shadows and canopy light absorption reduce direct sunlight beneath the crowns. The extra absorption is restrained so it does not compound the dense tree shadows into complete darkness. A small, normal-dependent leaf-transmission approximation adds dim daylight and sparse, soft sunflecks beneath overlapping opaque crowns. The patches project along the moving sun, drift slightly with wind and switch off entirely at night; they are shaded light contributions, not emissive ground or beam meshes. Slightly brighter cool ambient fill keeps the woods darker than camp while leaving trunks and ground readable. The shared cycle has 24 minutes of daylight and 8 minutes of night. The shadow camera follows the player's vicinity, retaining the 4096-pixel shadow map.

Mist integrates world-space density between camera and visible surface using the depth buffer. Twelve irregularly placed low pockets drift subtly through hollows and clearings. Nearby geometry occludes mist, the entrance has no mist blanket, and individual pockets remain light enough to see through. A stronger outer mist field hides the scenery boundary. Ordinary distance fog is sparse; darkness comes from shade and material colour rather than reducing the player's visible range with dense fog.

**Controls:** WASD walk, Shift sprint, Space jump, mouse look (or left-drag), Esc pause, R return to the forest entrance, E return to camp. Exploration does not spend resources or change quest progress.

Automated checks cover all trail routes with player collision, terrain/collision alignment, embedded roots, actual asset heights and crown clearance, natural scatter, allowed ground-cover types, mist height/locality and canopy shader integration. Browser WebGL appearance, interaction and FPS remain unverified because preview access is unavailable. No offline Blender render is used as evidence of final browser appearance.
